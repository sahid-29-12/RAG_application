Company Work-From-Home (WFH) Policy

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible for the hybrid work model. Contract workers must seek explicit approval from their line manager.

2. Hybrid Work Schedule
Employees are required to be in the office for a minimum of 3 days per week. The remaining 2 days may be worked remotely. Mandatory office days are Tuesdays and Thursdays. The third office day can be chosen by the employee with manager approval.

3. Working Hours
Core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone. Employees must be online and available for meetings during these core hours. Lunch breaks should be taken outside of core working hours if possible.

4. Equipment
The company will provide a standard company laptop and a one-time stipend of $500 for home office setup (e.g., monitor, ergonomic chair, desk). Employees are responsible for securing and maintaining a reliable high-speed internet connection at their own expense.

5. Security and Confidentiality
All remote work must be conducted over the company's secure VPN. Public Wi-Fi should only be used if connected through the VPN. Company documents must not be printed at home or left visible to unauthorized individuals. 

6. Leave and Absences
The WFH policy does not change existing paid time off (PTO) or sick leave policies. If an employee is unable to work due to illness, they must request sick leave through the standard HR portal, regardless of whether they were scheduled to work from home or the office that day.
